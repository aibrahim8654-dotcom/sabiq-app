Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built around the five pillars of daily Muslim discipline — simple enough to use every morning after Fajr.

🕌

Prayer Tracking

Log all 5 prayers with a single tap. Fajr is weighted highest — because we all know that's the hard one. When prayer time hits, the app can lock your phone from scrolling and remind you to pray.

📖

Quran Logging

Track your daily Quran reading by pages or minutes. Build a streak. Watch your friends' streaks. Stay consistent or face the consequences.

📿

Dhikr Check-In

One tap for morning adhkar. One tap for evening adhkar. That's it. Simple enough that you have no excuse to miss it.

🏆

Weekly Leaderboard

Live rankings among your friend circle. Resets every Monday so nobody falls hopelessly behind. The pressure is real. The motivation is real.

🔔

Anti-Scroll Prayer Reminder

When it's time to pray, the app locks your phone from endless scrolling and sends a calm reminder to get back to worship.

💪

Recovery Mode

Miss a day? We don't punish you — we bring you back. Recovery mode lets you earn back points instead of quitting. Consistency over perfection.
