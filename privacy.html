Privacy Policy

السابق

Last Updated: June 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Sabiq. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the Sabiq app — a discipline and accountability platform for Muslims built around prayer, Quran, dhikr, and healthy competition with friends.

Information We Collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect your email address. When you use the Sabiq app, we may collect:

• Your name and account details
• Prayer, Quran, and dhikr habit logs
• Leaderboard scores and streak data
• Friend circle membership and forfeit agreements
• Device information (device type, operating system)
• Usage patterns and preferences
• Information you voluntarily provide

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

• Provide and improve the Sabiq app
• Display your scores and ranking within your friend circles
• Send prayer reminders and leaderboard notifications
• Track habit streaks and calculate weekly points
• Send you updates about the app and new features
• Communicate with you about your account
• Improve our services based on usage patterns

Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored securely and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

• Access your personal data
• Request deletion of your data
• Opt out of communications
• Update your information at any time

Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Third-Party Services

We use Formspree for waitlist email collection. Please review their privacy policy for information about how they handle data. We do not use third-party advertising services.

Children's Privacy

Sabiq is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: sabiqapp@gmail.com
